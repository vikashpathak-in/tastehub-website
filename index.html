Meet the person behind TasteHub

Neha Kumari

Kitchen Owner & Food Creator

Neha is the heart behind TasteHub, a homemade vegetarian food venture built on her love for cooking and her desire to create something of her own.

Her approach combines homemade traditional recipes with fresh vegetarian cooking, creative food presentation, and a strong focus on quality and hygiene. She believes that good food is not just about taste; it is also about care, preparation, and the happiness of the people being served.

With a passion for exploring new things, Neha enjoys discovering ideas, trying new food preparations, and bringing variety to the TasteHub kitchen. Her aim is to make every meal feel thoughtfully prepared, fresh, and welcoming.
